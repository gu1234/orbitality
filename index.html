Orbitality

Get a feel for orbital mechanics by catching other spacecraft. Your first lesson: to catch up with something ahead of you in orbit, you slow down.

Friendly warning: your instincts learned physics from cars, bikes and shopping trolleys, and none of those orbit anything. Up here, speeding up makes you slower and braking gets you there sooner. Spend a few minutes at Flight School first and do exactly what it says, even when it sounds wrong. Especially when it sounds wrong.

    Hold a burn button to thrust. Longer holds burn harder. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to look around.
